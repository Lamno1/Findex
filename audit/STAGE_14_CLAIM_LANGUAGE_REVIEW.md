## Summary judgment on claim language

Of the words flagged for special attention in the brief ("persistent," "boundary
condition," "transportability," "information substitution," "information value," "credit
information," "complementarity," "stability," "replication," "registered," "effect,"
"mechanism"): this review did not find any instance in `body_stage8.tex` where these words
are used to assert more than the design supports. The two residual concerns are not
instances of overclaiming in the causal/mechanism sense the brief is most worried about;
they are **non-disclosure of population scope** (the module-administration selection) and
**imprecision of method description** (the specific bootstrap variant). Both are
addressable with additions, not retractions, and neither requires new estimation.
