# Stage 10 Reviewer-Objection Response Map

| Reviewer objection | Current evidence | What the manuscript can answer | What remains unresolved | Final wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-wave persistence may repeat selection/measurement | Negative gradient in both waves; qualified exposure; fixed historical moderator; LOO and bootstrap | It is less consistent with a 2024-only anomaly and documents recurring observed-pattern non-transportability | Underlying mechanism, selection and measurement cannot be separated | “Two-wave persistence reduces the one-wave-anomaly interpretation, but does not identify an economic mechanism.” |
| Firm and individual outcomes are not identical | Unit/outcome/moderator/technology differences are explicit | Those differences are the economic setting for the boundary-condition question | Whether the source is technology, supply, selection or measurement | “The firm-level relationship does not automatically generalise to individual formal borrowing.” |
| Coverage is too narrow for ‘credit-information environment’ | Coverage, depth and accessibility are separated | The result is specific to historical formal credit-reporting coverage | Broader institutional usability and lender use | Use the narrow moderator label consistently |
| Realised borrowing mixes demand and supply | Outcome definition and limitation are explicit | The paper reports an association with realised borrowing | Applications, approvals, lender type and terms | No information-value or supply mechanism claim |
| p>0.05 does not prove equality | Pooled triple p=0.305 | No statistically detectable wave difference under the pooled model | Exact equality | Never say identical or stable |

Stage 10 addresses the objection through claim refinement, not by pretending the current
evidence resolves it.
