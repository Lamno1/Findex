## Addendum (Stage 13, 2026-09-17)

The "substitution" row above originally read "RETAIN only as motivating **registered
prediction**." Stage 12's independent governance audit (`audit/STAGE_12_GOVERNANCE_AUDIT.md`)
found no pre-registration exists for the 93-economy/2021-2024 design; that phrase was
therefore itself a claim-language violation of the kind this document exists to catch. It is
corrected above (not silently rewritten — recorded here) to describe the prediction as
motivating an exploratory design, not a registered one. See
`audit/STAGE_13_COORDINATOR_PROVENANCE_DECISION_2026-09-17.md` for the full governance
finding and decision this correction implements.
