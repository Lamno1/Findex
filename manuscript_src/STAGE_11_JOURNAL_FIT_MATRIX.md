# Stage 11 — Journal Target & Positioning Audit

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: **Matrix completed; no journal selected and no submission authorized.**

## Manuscript identity used for screening

The paper is a non-causal, repeated-cross-section study of the observed
digital-payment/formal-borrowing association in Global Findex 2021 and 2024,
with historical 2019 formal credit-reporting coverage as a fixed moderator.
Its contribution is evidence about the cross-level transportability of an
observed firm-level pattern, not a causal mechanism or lender-underwriting
study.

## Search protocol

- Date searched: 2026-09-17.
- Primary sources: official publisher journal pages, aims-and-scope pages,
  author guidelines, and recent article pages.
- Recent-paper window: 2025–2026 where available.
- No quartile or ranking was used as a substitute for scope fit.
- A journal was not treated as a target merely because it publishes fintech or
  financial-inclusion papers.

## Recent-scope signals

- World Development currently publishes work on digital financial inclusion,
  fintech and financial exclusion, including a 2025 article using repeated
  surveys to study persistent spatial inequality in conventional and digital
  inclusion, and a 2025 article on cellphone banking and poverty.
- Journal of Banking & Finance recently published work on payment-generated
  data and BigTech platforms, illustrating that payment–information links are
  within scope but are often developed through formal models or platform data.
- Journal of Financial Services Research currently lists work on financial
  inclusion and fintech among its latest articles, alongside research on the
  demand, supply and operation of financial services.
- Financial Innovation's current articles and scope show an active interest in
  technology-enabled financial products and services, but the journal's
  portfolio is broad rather than limited to development economics.

## Literature/community fit

The manuscript should be positioned primarily in one conversation, with the
others used as supporting literatures:

1. **Primary:** financial inclusion and digital finance in development.
2. **Secondary:** consumer/formal borrowing and credit-information institutions.
3. **Conceptual bridge:** information value and cross-level transportability.

It should not present itself primarily as an information-economics or
financial-intermediation mechanism paper because lender use, application,
approval and loan terms are not observed.

## Current recommendation (not a submission decision)

The first serious shortlist should be built from:

1. Journal of Financial Services Research — strongest finance-specialist fit.
2. World Development — strongest development/general-interest fit.
3. Financial Innovation — strongest direct fintech fit.

Information Economics and Policy is a defensible conceptual alternative but
requires especially disciplined measurement language. Journal of Banking &
Finance and Journal of Development Economics remain stretch options rather
than default targets. Development Policy Review is conditional on a clearer
policy-facing version, and Electronic Markets is currently peripheral.

## What this audit does not decide

- No quartile-based target was selected.
- No submission order was fixed.
- No manuscript rewrite was made.
- No formatting conversion was made.
- Acceptance probability was not assessed.

The next decision should compare the top three against the complete author
guidelines, article limits, disclosure requirements and recent papers most
similar in design—not simply against rankings.
